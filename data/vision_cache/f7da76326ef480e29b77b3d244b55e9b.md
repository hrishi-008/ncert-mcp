How do we construct these congruent triangles, given the angle?

If A and B are marked such that OA = OB, and if C is chosen such that BC = AC, then by the SSS congruence condition, $\triangle OBC \cong \triangle OAC$. So we can bisect an angle as follows.

### Steps for Angle Bisection

1. Mark points A and B such that OA = OB.

[Figure: Angle XOY with O at the vertex, ray OX going up-right with point B marked by a small arc on it and X labelled further along, ray OY horizontal with point A marked by a small arc and Y labelled further along.]

2. Choosing any sufficiently long radius, cut arcs from A and B, keeping the radius same. Mark the point of intersection as C.
3. OC bisects $\angle AOB$.

[Figure: The same angle XOY with points O, B, X, A, Y; arcs drawn from A and B intersect at C, and a ray from O through C is drawn, bisecting the angle.]

So, a $45°$ angle can be constructed by first constructing a $90°$ angle and then bisecting it.
