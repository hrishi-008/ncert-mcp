2. **Wavy Wave**

Construct this.

[Figure: A wave made of two shaded semicircles, filled with vertical lines. The first semicircle sits above a horizontal central line on the left, and the second semicircle hangs below the central line on the right, joined where the first ends.]

As the length of the central line is not specified, we can take it to be of any length.

Let us take AB to be the central line such that the length of AB is 8 cm. We write this as AB = 8 cm.

Here, the first wave is drawn as a half circle.

[Figure: A compass with a pencil, with a double-headed arrow below it labelled "?". Beside it, a line AB with a half circle drawn above it from A to a point X on the line; a double-headed arrow below AX labelled "?" and a longer double-headed arrow below AB labelled "8 cm". Labels: A, X, B.]

### Figure it Out

1. What radius should be taken in the compass to get this half circle? What should be the length of AX?
2. Take a central line of a different length and try to draw the wave on it.
3. Try to recreate the figure where the waves are smaller than a half circle (as appearing in the neck of the figure, 'A Person'). The challenge here is to get both the waves to be identical. This may be tricky!
