How do we proceed with this problem? Here is a method.

> ? What are the measures of $\angle B$ and $\angle Y$?

We know that the sum of the angles of a triangle is $180°$.
So $\angle B + 35° + 75° = 180°$,
or $\angle B + 110° = 180°$
Thus, $\angle B = 70°$.
Similarly, $\angle Y$ is also $70°$.
Thus, we have $\angle B = \angle Y$.

> ? Does this help in showing that $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle XYZ$ are congruent?

[Figure: Two triangles side by side. Left triangle ABC with angle 35° at A, angle 70° at B, angle 75° at C, side BC = 4 cm. Right triangle XYZ with angle 35° at X, angle 70° at Y, angle 75° at Z, side YZ = 4 cm.]

These two triangles now satisfy the ASA condition with

$\angle B = \angle Y$

$BC = YZ$

$\angle C = \angle Z$

So, $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle XYZ$.
