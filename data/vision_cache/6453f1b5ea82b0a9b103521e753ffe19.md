We have seen that when two lines are parallel, the internal angles on the same side of the transversal add up to $180°$. So $\angle B = 140°$.

So, for what values of $\angle B$, does a triangle not exist? Does the length AB play any part here?

From the discussion above, it can be seen that the length AB does not play any part in deciding the existence of a triangle. We can say that a triangle does not exist when $\angle B$ is greater than or equal to $140°$.
