### Figure it Out

1. Fill in the blanks with numbers, and boxes with operation signs such that the expressions on both sides are equal.

   (a) $24 + (6 - 4) = 24 + 6$ [Figure: empty box] __________

   (b) $38 + ($__________ [Figure: empty box] ________$) = 38 + 9 - 4$

   (c) $24 - (6 + 4) = 24$ [Figure: empty box] $6 - 4$

   (d) $24 - 6 - 4 = 24 - 6$ [Figure: empty box] __________

   (e) $27 - (8 + 3) = 27$ __________ $8$ __________ $3$

   (f) $27 - ($__________ [Figure: empty box] ________$) = 27 - 8 + 3$

**Ans:**

   (a) $24 + (6 - 4) = 24 + 6$ [Figure: box containing a minus sign] $\underline{4}$

   (b) $38 + (\underline{9}$ [Figure: box containing a minus sign] $\underline{4}) = 38 + 9 - 4$

   (c) $24 - (6 + 4) = 24$ [Figure: box containing a minus sign] $6 - 4$

   (d) $24 - 6 - 4 = 24 - 6$ [Figure: box containing a minus sign] $\underline{4}$

   (e) $27 - (8 + 3) = 27 - 8 - 3$

   (f) $27 - (\underline{8}$ [Figure: box containing a minus sign] $\underline{3}) = 27 - 8 + 3$

2. Remove the brackets and write the expression having the same value.

   (a) $14 + (12 + 10)$

   (b) $14 - (12 + 10)$

   (c) $14 + (12 - 10)$

   (d) $14 - (12 - 10)$

   (e) $-14 + 12 - 10$

   (f) $14 - (-12 - 10)$

   **Ans:** (a) and (f)
