### Mind the Mistake, Mend the Mistake!

A student used a protractor to measure the angles as shown below. In each figure, identify the incorrect usage(s) of the protractor and discuss how the reading could have been made and think how it can be corrected.

[Figure: Protractor placed upright on a horizontal base with its centre at point U; two red rays drawn from U, one going up to the right through the scale and one going down to the right below the base. Label: ∠U = 35°]

[Figure: Protractor tilted, centre at point V; two red rays from V, one going up to the right and one going down to the right along the lower edge of the tilted protractor. Label: ∠V = 80°]

[Figure: Protractor placed upright on a horizontal base; two red rays forming an angle from a vertex at the centre, one passing up to the right beyond the scale and the other a ray ending at the vertex of the angle drawn above the protractor, forming a bent line. Label: ∠W = 70°]

[Figure: Protractor tilted, with two red rays from a point near the centre of the protractor's base; one ray goes to the left along the tilted base and one ray goes slightly above it to the left. Label: ∠X = 150°]

[Figure: Protractor turned sideways with its straight edge vertical on the left; point Y marked at the left of the centre; two red rays from the centre, one going up and slightly right and one going down and slightly right. Label: ∠Y = 120°]

[Figure: Protractor turned upside down, with its straight edge at the top; two red rays from the centre point, one going to the right slightly upward and one going down and slightly left. Label: ∠Z = 85°]
