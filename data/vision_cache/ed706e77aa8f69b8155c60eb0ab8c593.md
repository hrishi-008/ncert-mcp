### Figure it out

1. How many different magic squares can be made using the numbers 1 – 9?

**Ans:** There is exactly one unique magic square with numbers 1 – 9, if we ignore rotations and reflections.

[Figure: 3×3 magic square with rows: 8 1 6 / 3 5 7 / 4 9 2]

| 8 | 1 | 6 |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 5 | 7 |
| 4 | 9 | 2 |
