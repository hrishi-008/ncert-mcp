(ii) Now, we need to multiply $\frac{2}{20}$ by 3.

Distance covered in $\frac{3}{4}$ of an hour $= 3 \times \frac{2}{20}$

$= \frac{6}{20}$.

So, $\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{2}{5} = \frac{6}{20} = \frac{3}{10}$.
