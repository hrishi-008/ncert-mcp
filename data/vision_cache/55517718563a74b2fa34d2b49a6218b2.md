Similarly, the sides AB and BC are equal. So $\angle A = \angle C$.

So, all the three angles of an equilateral triangle are equal, just like their sides.

> ?

What could be their measures?

As the three angles should add up to $180°$, we have

$3 \times$ angle in an equilateral triangle $= 180°$.

So each angle is $60°$.

> ?

Verify this by construction.

Thus, just using the notion of congruence, we have deduced that the angles of an equilateral triangle are all $60°$.
