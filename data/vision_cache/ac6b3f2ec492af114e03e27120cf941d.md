> ### Math Talk

Is it possible to draw $\overrightarrow{OC}$ such that the two angles are equal to each other in size?

### Let's Explore

We can try to solve this problem using a piece of paper. Recall that when a fold is made, it creates a crease which is straight.

Take a rectangular piece of paper and on one of its sides, mark the straight angle AOB. By folding, try to get a line (crease) passing through O that divides $\angle$AOB into two equal angles.

How can it be done?
