## 8.3 Constructing Squares and Rectangles

Now, let us start constructing squares and rectangles. How would you construct a square with a side of 6 cm?

For help, you can see the following figures. A square PQRS of side length 6 cm is constructed.

[Figure: A horizontal line segment with endpoints P (left) and Q (right), labelled "6 cm" between them.]

**STEP 2**

[Figure: A protractor placed on line segment PQ with its centre at P; PQ is labelled "6 cm", with a small mark at the 90° position above P.]

Mark a point to draw a perpendicular to PQ through P.

**STEP 3**
**Method 1**

[Figure: A horizontal segment PQ labelled "6 cm" and a vertical segment through P going up to a point S, labelled "6 cm"; a small square at P marks the angle "90°".]

Mark S on the perpendicular such that PS = 6 cm using a ruler.

This can also be done using a compass.
