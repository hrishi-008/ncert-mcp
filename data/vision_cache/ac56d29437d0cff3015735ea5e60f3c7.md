### Simplify each of the following expressions:

(a) $p + p + p + p,\; p + p + p + q,\; p + q + p - q$
(b) $p - q + p - q,\; p + q - p + q$
(c) $p + q - (p + q),\; p - q - p - q$
(d) $2d - d - d - d,\; 2d - d - d - c$
(e) $2d - d - (d - c),\; 2d - (d - d) - c$

**Ans:**
