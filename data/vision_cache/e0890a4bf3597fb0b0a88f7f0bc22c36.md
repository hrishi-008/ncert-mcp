## 6.1 Numbers Tell us Things

**?** What do the numbers in the figure below tell us?

Remember the children from the Grade 6 textbook of mathematics? Now, they call out numbers using a different rule.

[Figure: Seven stick-figure children of different heights standing in a row against ruled horizontal lines, each with a speech bubble. From left to right the numbers called out are 0, 1, 0, 2, 2, 2, 1.]

**?** What do you think these numbers mean?

The children rearrange themselves and each one says a number based on the new arrangement.

[Figure: Seven stick-figure children standing in a new row against ruled horizontal lines, each with a speech bubble. From left to right the numbers called out are 0, 0, 1, 2, 2, 5, 6.]
