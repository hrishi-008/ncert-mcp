Find the factors of 225 using prime factorisation.

Factorising 225 to its primes, we get
$225 = 5 \times 5 \times 3 \times 3$.

[Figure: Prime factorisation of 225 by repeated division: 5 | 225; 5 | 45; 3 | 9; 3 | 3; remainder 1.]

We have seen that any 'subpart' of this factorisation gives us a factor. Let us systematically form these subparts.

Prime factors: 3, 5.

Combination of two prime factors: $3 \times 3 = 9$, $5 \times 5 = 25$, $3 \times 5 = 15$.

Combination of three prime factors: $3 \times 3 \times 5 = 45$, $3 \times 5 \times 5 = 75$.

Combination of four prime factors: $3 \times 3 \times 5 \times 5 = 225$. Adding 1 to this list of factors, we see that the factors of 225 are 1, 3, 5, 9, 15, 25, 45, 75, 225.
