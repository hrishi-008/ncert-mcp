### Math Talk

> Math Talk

Can you give a simple rule to divide any number by a number of the form 1 followed by zeroes — 10, 100, 1000, etc.? For example, $\frac{123}{10}$, $\frac{24}{100}$ or $\frac{678}{1000}$ ? Look for a pattern in the previous problems.

Here is one such rule. Let us consider the example $123 \div 10$.

**Step 1:** Write the dividend as it is and place a decimal point at the end.

123.

**Step 2:** Count the number of zeroes in the divisor.

10 → 1 zero

**Step 3:** Move the decimal point from Step 1 left by the same number of places as the count from Step 2. Add zeroes in front if needed.
