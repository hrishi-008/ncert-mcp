### Construct

**Q.1.** Draw a rectangle with sides of length 4 cm and 6 cm. After drawing, check if it satisfies both the rectangle properties.

**Ans.** $\angle A = \angle B = \angle C = \angle D = 90°$

$AB = CD = 4$ cm & $AD = BC = 6$ cm.

[Figure: A rectangle ABCD with A at bottom left, B at bottom right, C at top right, D at top left. Right-angle marks at A and B. Bottom side AB is labelled "4 cm" with arrows; right side BC is labelled "6 cm" with arrows.]

**Q.2.** Draw a rectangle of sides 2 cm and 10 cm. After drawing, check if it satisfies both the rectangle properties.

**Ans.**

$\angle P = \angle Q = \angle R = \angle S = 90°$
$PQ = SR = 10$ cm & $PS = QR = 2$ cm

[Figure: A rectangle PQRS with P at bottom left, Q at bottom right, R at top right, S at top left. Right-angle marks at P and Q. Bottom side PQ is labelled "10 cm" with arrows; right side QR is labelled "2 cm" with arrows.]

**Q.3.** Is it possible to construct a 4-sided figure in which—
- all the angles are equal to $90°$ but
- opposite sides are not equal?

Ans. No.
