Let us focus, for the moment, only on the row sums. We have seen that in a $3 \times 3$ grid with numbers $1 - 9$, the total of row sums will always be 45. Since in a magic square the row sums are all equal, and they add up to 45, they have to be 15 each. Thus, we have the following observation.

**Observation 1:** In a magic square made using the numbers $1 - 9$, the magic sum must be 15.
