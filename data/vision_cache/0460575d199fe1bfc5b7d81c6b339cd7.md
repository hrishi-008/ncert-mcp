### Construction of a 60° angle

> ?

How do we construct a 60° angle?

We get a 60° angle if we construct an equilateral triangle! We can use the following steps for this.

Suppose we need a 60° angle at point A on a line segment AX.

**Step 1**

[Figure: A horizontal line segment from A to X, with an arc centred at A of any radius that cuts the segment at point B. Labels: A, B, X.]

Construct an arc with centre A and any radius.

**Step 2**

[Figure: Line segment AX with the arc from B, and a second arc of the same radius drawn from B that meets the first arc at point C. A line from A through C is drawn upward. Labels: C, A, B, X.]

With the same radius, cut another arc from B that meets the first arc. Let C be the point at which the arcs meet.

We have $\angle$CAX = 60°.
