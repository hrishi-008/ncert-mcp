### Classifying Angles

Angles are classified in three groups as shown below. Right angles are shown in the second group. What could be the common feature of the other two groups?

[Figure: A box divided into three columns. The first column contains several angles each smaller than a right angle (acute angles) in different orientations. The second column contains several right angles in different orientations. The third column contains several angles greater than a right angle but less than a straight angle (obtuse angles) in different orientations.]

In the first group, all angles are less than a right angle or in other words, less than a quarter turn. Such angles are called **acute angles**.

In the third group, all angles are greater than a right angle but less than a straight angle. The turning is more than a quarter turn and less than a half turn. Such angles are called **obtuse angles**.
