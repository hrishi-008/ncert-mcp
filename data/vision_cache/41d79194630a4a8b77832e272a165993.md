### Can you draw a figure with radial arms that has a) exactly 5 angles of symmetry, b) 6 angles of symmetry? Also find the angles of symmetry in each case.

*Hint:* Use 5 radial arms for the first case. What should the angle between two adjacent radial arms be?

[Figure: (a) Five narrow wedge-shaped radial arms meeting at a central point, evenly spaced around it, one pointing straight up. Each arm has a dashed line along its middle, extended slightly beyond the arm's outer end. Label "(a)".]

**Ans.**

Angles of symmetry = $72°$, $144°$, $216°$, $288°$, $360°$.

[Figure: (b) Six petal-shaped (leaf-shaped) radial arms meeting at a central point, evenly spaced, one pointing straight up and one straight down. Each petal has a dashed line along its middle, extended slightly beyond the tip. Label "(b)".]

Angles of symmetry = $60°$, $120°$, $180°$, $240°$, $300°$, $360°$.
