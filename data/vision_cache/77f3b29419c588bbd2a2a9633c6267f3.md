**Does adding the terms of an expression in any order give the same value? Take some more expressions and check. Consider expressions with more than 3 terms also.**

**Ans:** Yes, adding the terms of an expression in any order gives the same value Consider an expression with 3 terms

$9 + 4 + 6$

**Order 1:** $9 + 4 + 6$

$= (9 + 4) + 6 = 13 + 6 = 19$

**Order 2:** $9 + 4 + 6$

$= 9 + (4 + 6) = 9 + 10 = 19$
