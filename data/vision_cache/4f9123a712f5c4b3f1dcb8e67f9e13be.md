**Example 11:** What is the value of $0.06 \div 5$?

$0.06 \rightarrow$ 0 Ones + 0 Tenths + 6 Hundredths
0 Ones $\div$ 5 $\rightarrow$ 0 Ones.

When we move from Ones to Tenths we need to place the decimal point in the quotient.
0 Tenths $\div$ 5 $\rightarrow$ 0 Tenths.

6 Hundredths $\div$ 5 $\rightarrow$ 1 Hundredths, and 1 Hundredth remains. We need to regroup 1 Hundredth into 10 Thousandths.

10 Thousandths $\div$ 5 $\rightarrow$ 2 Thousandths.

So, the quotient is 0.012.
