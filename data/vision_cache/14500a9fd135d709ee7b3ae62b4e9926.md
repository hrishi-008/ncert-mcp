### Using tokens for subtraction

We have seen how to perform addition of integers with positive tokens and negative tokens. We can also perform subtraction using tokens!

**Example:** Let us subtract: (+5) – (+4).

> [Figure: Five green positive tokens, the first four crossed out, the last one not crossed out.]
> $(+5)-(+4)=+1$

This is easy to do. From 5 positives take away 4 positives to see the result.

**Example:** Let us subtract: (–7) – (–5).

> [Figure: Seven orange negative tokens, the first five crossed out, the last two not crossed out.]
> (–7) – (–5) = –2
