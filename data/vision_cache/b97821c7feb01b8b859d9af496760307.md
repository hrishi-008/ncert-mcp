?  How many m is (a) 10 cm? (b) 15 cm?

10 cm = $\frac{1}{10}$ m = 0.1 m

Since each cm is one-hundredth of a meter, 15 cm can be written as

15 cm = $\frac{15}{100}$ m

= $\frac{10}{100}$ m + $\frac{5}{100}$ m

= $\frac{1}{10}$ m + $\frac{5}{100}$ m

= 0.15 m.
