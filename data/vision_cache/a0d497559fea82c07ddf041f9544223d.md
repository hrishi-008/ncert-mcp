## 3.4 Decimal Place Value

You may have noticed that whenever we need to measure something more accurately, we split a part into 10 (smaller) equal parts — we split a unit into 10 one-tenths and then split each one-tenth into 10, one-hundredths and then we use these smaller parts to measure.
