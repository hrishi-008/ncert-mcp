For the windmill, the angles of symmetry are $90°$ (quarter turn), $180°$ (half turn), $270°$ (three-quarter turn) and $360°$ (full turn). Observe that when any figure is rotated by $360°$, it comes back to its original position, so $360°$ is always an angle of symmetry.

Thus, we see that the windmill has 4 angles of symmetry.
