In a day, the number of times —

(a) the first fountain will fill the cistern is $1 \div 1 = 1$

(b) the second fountain will fill the cistern is $1 \div \frac{1}{2} =$ _____

**Ans:** 2 times

(c) the third fountain will fill the cistern is $1 \div \frac{1}{4} =$ _____

**Ans:** 4 times

(d) the fourth fountain will fill the cistern is $1 \div \frac{1}{5} =$ _____

**Ans:** 5 times

The number of times the four fountains together will fill the cistern in a day is ___ + ____ + ___ + ____ = 12.

**Ans:** The number of times the four fountains together will fill the cistern in a day is
$= 1 + 2 + 4 + 5 = 12$
