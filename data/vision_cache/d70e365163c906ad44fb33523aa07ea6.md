## 3.5 Units of Measurement

### Length Conversion

We have been using a scale to measure length for a few years. We already know that 1 cm = 10 mm (millimeters).

How many cm is 1 mm?

$1 \text{ mm} = \frac{1}{10} \text{ cm} = 0.1 \text{ cm}$ (i.e., one-tenth of a cm).

How many cm is (a) 5 mm? (b) 12 mm?

$5 \text{ mm} = \frac{5}{10} \text{ cm} = 0.5 \text{ cm}$

$12 \text{ mm} = 10 \text{ mm} + 2 \text{ mm}$

$= 1 \text{ cm} + \frac{2}{10} \text{ cm}$

$= 1.2 \text{ cm}.$
