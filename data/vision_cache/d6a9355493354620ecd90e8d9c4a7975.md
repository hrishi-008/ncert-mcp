Therefore, $\angle XAB = 50°$, and $\angle YAC = 70°$. Can we find $\angle BAC$ from this? We know that $\angle XAB$, $\angle YAC$ and $\angle BAC$ together form $180°$. So

$$\angle XAB + \angle YAC + \angle BAC = 180°$$

$$50° + \angle BAC + 70° = 180°$$

$$120° + \angle BAC = 180°$$

Thus, $\angle BAC = 60°$

Now construct a triangle (taking BC to be of any suitable length) and verify if this is indeed the case.
