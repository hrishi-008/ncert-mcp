## Parity of Expressions

Consider the algebraic expression: $3n + 4$. For different values of $n$, the expression has different parity:

| $n$ | Value of $3n + 4$ | Parity of the Value |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 13 | odd |
| 8 | 28 | even |
| 10 | 34 | even |
