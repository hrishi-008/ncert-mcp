### Figure it Out

**Punching Game**

The fold is a line of symmetry. Punch holes at different locations of a folded square sheet of paper using a punching machine and create different symmetric patterns.

[Figure: Three images in a row. First, a folded square sheet of paper. Second, the folded sheet with one hole punched near the fold. Third, the unfolded square sheet showing two circular holes placed symmetrically on either side of the fold line.]
