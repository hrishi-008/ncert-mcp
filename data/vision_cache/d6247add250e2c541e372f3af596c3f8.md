## Parallel and Intersecting Lines

**Page No. 107**

1. **Can two straight lines intersect at more than one point?**

   **Ans:** No

2. **What patterns do you observe among these angles?**

   **Ans:**

   $\angle a = \angle c$ and $\angle b = \angle d$.

   $\angle a + \angle b = 180°$ and $\angle b + \angle c = 180°$.

   Find more such relations.

3. **Is this always true for any pair of intersecting lines?**

   **Ans:** Yes
