**Obtuse Angle:** Angles that are greater than the right angle and less than the straight angle, i.e., greater than $90°$ and less than $180°$, are called **obtuse** angles.

[Figure: Two obtuse angles. Left: ray through X pointing left from vertex I, with another ray through S pointing down and to the right; the angle at I is marked $110°$. Right: ray through T pointing left from vertex W, with another ray through S pointing up and to the right; the angle at W is marked $130°$.]

Have we covered all the possible measures that an angle can take? Here is another type of angle.

**Reflex angle:** Angles that are greater than the straight angle and less than the whole angle, i.e., greater than $180°$ and less than $360°$, are called **reflex** angles.

[Figure: Two reflex angles. Right: vertex M with one ray through T pointing left and another ray through S pointing down and to the left; the reflex angle is marked by an arc going around the outside of M.]
