2. Construct a rectangle where one of its sides is 5 cm and the length of a diagonal is 7 cm.

*Solution*

Let us draw a rough diagram.

Let us decide the steps of construction. Which line can be drawn first?

> [Figure: A rough rectangle ABCD with A at top left, B at top right, C at bottom right, D at bottom left. The diagonal DB is marked 7 cm and the base DC is marked 5 cm.]

**STEP 1**

The base CD measuring length 5 cm can be easily constructed.

[Figure: A horizontal line segment with endpoints D (left) and C (right), labelled 5 cm.]

Next?

**STEP 2**

Draw a perpendicular to line DC at the point C. Let us call this line $l$.

[Figure: A horizontal segment DC labelled 5 cm, with a vertical line $l$ drawn upward from C, perpendicular to DC.]

This is easy as we know that this line is perpendicular to the base. The point B should be somewhere on this line $l$.

How do we spot it? What else do we know about the position of B?

We know that it is at a distance of 7 cm from the point D.
