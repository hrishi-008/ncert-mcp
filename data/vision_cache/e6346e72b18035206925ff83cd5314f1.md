You might have developed your own method. Here is one simple approach.

### Steps of Construction to Copy an Angle

1. [Figure: Step 1. On the left, an angle with vertex A, one ray going up and to the right and the other ray horizontal to the right. On the right, a horizontal ray starting at point X.]

2. [Figure: Step 2. On the left, the angle with vertex A has an arc drawn from A, crossing the slanted ray at B and the horizontal ray at C. On the right, a horizontal ray starting at X.]

   Draw an arc from A.
   This gives us three points that form the isosceles triangle $\triangle ABC$.

3. [Figure: Step 3. On the left, the angle at A with the arc meeting the rays at B and C. On the right, an arc of the same radius drawn from X, crossing the horizontal ray at Z.]

   Draw an arc of the same radius from X.

4. [Figure: Step 4. On the left, the angle at A with the arc meeting the rays at B and C. On the right, the arc from X crossing the horizontal ray at Z, with a second small arc crossing it at point Y above Z.]

   Measure BC using a compass. Transfer this length on the arc from Z to get YZ = BC.
