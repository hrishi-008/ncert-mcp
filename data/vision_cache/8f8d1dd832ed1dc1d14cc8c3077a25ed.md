We know that the fraction $\frac{5}{10}$ can be represented as a decimal 0.5.

So, each girl will get 14 m and an additional 0.5 m of ribbon.

Hence, the length of ribbon each will get is 14 + 0.5 = 14.5 m.

> [?] Now, what if the ribbon was shared between four friends instead of 2?

So, each will get 29 ÷ 4 m, that is $\frac{29}{4}$ m.

Now, the denominator of the fraction is 4. To convert a fraction to a decimal, it helps if the denominator is of the form 1, 10, 100, 1000, and so on. Can we find a fraction equivalent to $\frac{29}{4}$ with such a denominator?

Is 4 a factor of 10? No. Is it a factor of 100? Yes. 4 × 25 = 100. So we can get an equivalent fraction of $\frac{29}{4}$ by multiplying the numerator and denominator by 25.

$$\frac{29 \times 25}{4 \times 25} = \frac{725}{100} = 7.25$$

So each of the 4 friends will get 7.25 m of ribbon.
