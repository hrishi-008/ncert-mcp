2. For each of the questions below, choose the expression that describes the solution. Then simplify it.

    (a) Maria bought 8 m of lace to decorate the bags she made for school. She used $\frac{1}{4}$ m for each bag and finished the lace. How many bags did she decorate?

    (i) $8 \times \frac{1}{4}$  (ii) $\frac{1}{8} \times \frac{1}{4}$

    (iii) $8 \div \frac{1}{4}$  (iv) $\frac{1}{4} \div 8$

    (b) $\frac{1}{2}$ meter of ribbon is used to make 8 badges. What is the length of the ribbon used for each badge?

    (i) $8 \times \frac{1}{2}$  (ii) $\frac{1}{2} \div \frac{1}{8}$

    (iii) $8 \div \frac{1}{2}$  (iv) $\frac{1}{2} \div 8$

    (c) A baker needs $\frac{1}{6}$ kg of flour to make one loaf of bread. He has 5 kg of flour. How many loaves of bread can he make?

    (i) $5 \times \frac{1}{6}$  (ii) $\frac{1}{6} \div 5$

    (iii) $5 \div \frac{1}{6}$  (iv) $5 \times 6$
