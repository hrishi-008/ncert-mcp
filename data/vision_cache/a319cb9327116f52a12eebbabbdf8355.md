**Example 17:** Given $53 \times 18 = 954$. Find out $63 \times 18$.

As $63 \times 18$ means 63 times 18,

$63 \times 18 = (53 + 10) \times 18$

$= 53 \times 18 + 10 \times 18$

$= 954 + 180$

$= 1134.$
