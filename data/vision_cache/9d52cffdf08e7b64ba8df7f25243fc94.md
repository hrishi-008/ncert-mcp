**Example 8:** Find the value of $1324 \div 4$.

$1324 \div 4 \rightarrow$ Divide 1324 into 4 equal parts.

1 Thousand + 3 Hundreds + 2 Tens + 4 Ones

[Figure: A tree diagram with four arrows from "1 Thousand + 3 Hundreds + 2 Tens + 4 Ones" pointing to four empty rounded boxes.]

1 Thousand $\div$ 4 $\rightarrow$ Not possible without regrouping.

Regroup 1 Thousand into 10 Hundreds.

10 Hundreds + 3 Hundreds = 13 Hundreds.

13 Hundreds + 2 Tens + 4 Ones

[Figure: A tree diagram with four arrows from "13 Hundreds + 2 Tens + 4 Ones" (13 Hundreds in red) pointing to four empty rounded boxes.]

13 Hundreds $\div$ 4 $\rightarrow$ Each part gets 3 Hundreds, and 1 Hundred remains.

13 Hundreds + 2 Tens + 4 Ones

[Figure: A tree diagram with five arrows from "13 Hundreds + 2 Tens + 4 Ones" (13 Hundreds in red) pointing to four boxes labelled "3 Hundreds", "3 Hundreds", "3 Hundreds", "3 Hundreds" and a fifth label "1 Hundred" (not in a box).]

[Figure: Long division beside the tree, with column headings Th, H, T, O: 4) 1324 (0 + 3 + ___ + ___, with the digits 13 in red; below it "–12", a line, and "1".]

Regroup 1 Hundred into 10 Tens.

10 Tens + 2 Tens = 12 Tens.
